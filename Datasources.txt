Economic Factors					
Economic Openness				Economic openness, in political economy, the degree to which nondomestic transactions (imports and exports) take place and affect the size and growth of a national economy. The degree of openness is measured by the actual size of registered imports and exports within a national economy, also known as the Impex rate	https://li.com/research/global-index-of-economic-openness/
Trade Openness				Trade openness is one measure of the extent to which a country is engaged in the global trading system. Trade openness is usually measured by the ratio between the sum of exports and imports and gross domestic product (GDP).	https://ourworldindata.org/grapher/trade-openness
Taxation Policy/Competitiveness				The International Tax Competitiveness Index (ITCI) seeks to measure the extent to which a country’s tax system adheres to two important aspects of tax policy: competitiveness and neutrality.	https://taxfoundation.org/publications/international-tax-competitiveness-index/
Oil Reserves Stockpiles				Measure of strategic reserves of oil pet county in barrells as well as a % of world share	https://www.worldometers.info/oil/oil-reserves-by-country/
Inflation Forecast				Inflation forecast is measured in terms of the consumer price index (CPI) or harmonised index of consumer prices (HICP) for euro area countries, the euro area aggregate and the United Kingdom. Inflation measures the general evolution of prices. It is defined as the change in the prices of a basket of goods and services that are typically purchased by households. Projections are based on an assessment of the economic climate in individual countries and the world economy, using a combination of model-based analyses and expert judgement. The indicator is expressed in annual growth rates.	https://data.oecd.org/price/inflation-forecast.htm
Current Inflation				inflation is a general increase in the prices of goods and services in an economy. When the general price level rises, each unit of currency buys fewer goods and services; consequently, inflation corresponds to a reduction in the purchasing power of money.	https://data.worldbank.org/indicator/FP.CPI.TOTL.ZG
Stock Price Volatility				Volatility is the rate at which the price of a stock increases or decreases over a particular period. Higher stock price volatility often means higher risk and helps an investor to estimate the fluctuations that may happen in the future.	https://www.theglobaleconomy.com/rankings/Stock_price_volatility/

Climate Change Vulnerability Index				A country's ND-GAIN index score is composed of a Vulnerability score and a Readiness score. Vulnerability measures a country's exposure, sensitivity and ability to adapt to the negative impact of climate change. ND-GAIN measures the overall vulnerability by considering vulnerability in six life-supporting sectors – food, water, health, ecosystem service, human habitat and infrastructure	https://gain-new.crc.nd.edu/ranking/vulnerability
Food Security Risk Index				The Global Food Security Index (GFSI) is the pre-eminent source of intelligence on the drivers of global food security. Developed by Economist Impact and supported by Corteva Agriscience, it evaluates food security in 113 countries across four key pillars: affordability, availability, quality and safety, and sustainability and adaptation. The index is based on a dynamic benchmarking model constructed from 68 qualitative and quantitative drivers of food security.	https://impact.economist.com/sustainability/project/food-security-index/
