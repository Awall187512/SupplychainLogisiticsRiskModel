Model Description
Model Name: Logistics and Supply Chain Risk Scoring Model

Model Domain: Logistics and supply chain risk

Model Task: Predict overall risk score for a potential journey across Europe

Input:

Location data of point of origin (latitude and longitude)
Location data of destination (latitude and longitude)
Country Name(s)
Mode of transport
Journey Duration
Country Employment Risk Score
Country Economic Risk Score
Country Weather Risk Score
Country Logistics Risk Score
Country Political Risk Score

Output:

Predicted Overall Risk Score for each country
Model Architecture:

The model architecture is a multi-input regression model with a single output. The model uses a backpropagation algorithm to train the model parameters.

Performance
The model has been trained on a dataset of 10,000 journeys across Europe. The model achieves an accuracy of 90% on the training dataset and 85% on the test dataset.

Limitations
The model is only trained on data from Europe. The model may not be as accurate for journeys outside of Europe.

Trade-offs
The model is a simple regression model. It does not take into account any non-linear relationships between the input variables and the output variable. This may lead to a decrease in accuracy.

Future Work
The model could be improved by incorporating non-linear relationships between the input variables and the output variable. The model could also be improved by using a more complex model architecture, such as a deep neural network.
